# THE FUTURE SPEAKS EVERY LANGUAGE

### IMAGINE 2045.

A child asks a question in the language she speaks at home.

Her tutor understands.

Not because every village got a new building, but because affordable AI learning tools, local teachers and public access finally worked together.

Later, she uses what she learns to build a simple water-quality sensor for her community.

The machine didn't have the dream.

**It helped make sure the dream was never lost in translation.**
